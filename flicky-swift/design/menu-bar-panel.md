# Menu bar panel

## Direction contract

THESIS: Match the user-supplied translucent Flicky reference within a native macOS panel.
OWN-WORLD: Charcoal glass, fine illuminated edges, 19-point outer corners, white system typography, mint status and safe-to-spend accents.
STORY: See the connected account and available balance, then ask Flicky for help.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 480-point panel; header, paired account tiles, centered 44-point balance, safe-to-spend row, actual bill chips, question composer, two suggestions, and compact voice/model/account footer.
FORM: User-pinned reference composition; no alternative direction needed. Operate mode.
FINISH: Native compilation and screenshot inspection, independent review, and scoped design documentation.

## Product constraints

Nessie supplies the account; no Plaid or Google authentication is represented. The email tile displays the login label. Merchant chips use live recurring/upcoming bills; their count and labels vary by account. The voice shortcut remains Control–Option. Full insights, refresh, dismiss, and quit remain in the options menu. Text questions use the existing response pipeline.
